Score tracker for **Scopa**, the Italian card game. Tap **+ Scopa** during the round, then pick who took Carte, Denari, Settebello and Primiera.

**[Open the app →](https://jan-arn.github.io/scopa-tracker/)** · on iPhone: Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*

German & English · works offline · no build, just `index.html`
